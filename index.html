About Musmato

Established in 2009.
Built on decades of experience.

Musmato B.V. has operated as a security, technology and software consultancy since 2009. Founded by Pieter Claassen, it connects senior cybersecurity and risk-management experience with applied research and hands-on software engineering.

Our background spans financial services, mobility, energy and cloud-based digital products. Today, we focus on fractional security leadership, AI and security research, agent-assisted software engineering and our risk-assessment product, Riskonami.

Musmato engages directly with organisations and agrees scope, delivery responsibilities and resourcing for each assignment.

Our vision

AI should make technology more useful to people: improving productivity, helping organisations work more efficiently and creating lasting economic value.

Musmato aims to make that progress sustainable through responsible AI adoption, sound security practices and systems that remain manageable over time.
